What this site collects about you

On its own, nothing. There's no analytics and no advertising code here, and I don't store any cookies of my own in your browser. You don't have to click through anything, because there's nothing to allow. The only exception is the email updates signup below, and that runs outside this site and only if you sign up yourself.

Fonts, images and styles load from this site, not from Google or anywhere else. So your IP address isn't sent to any third party.

What's embedded here from elsewhere

  • The video is from YouTube in no-cookie mode. Until you click it, nothing is sent anywhere. Once you play it, Google learns your IP address and that you played it. From then on Google's policies apply.
  • Server. The site runs on GitHub Pages. The server provider sees visitors' IP addresses in technical logs, just like every web server in the world. I don't have access to them.

Where the links on this site lead

To Skool, Calendly, Instagram and my email. Once you click, you're on their side and their rules apply, not mine.

If you enter something about yourself there, for example you pick a call time on Calendly or message me on Instagram, it reaches me. I use it only to get back to you and to have that call with you. I keep it for as long as it makes sense, I don't sell it to anyone and I don't pass it on anywhere.

Emails about the sourcing map

If you subscribe, I save your email and your region, if you fill it in. I use them only to write to you when new places are added to the sourcing map, or new places near you. I won't send you anything else and I won't give them to anyone.

  • The legal basis is your consent, which you give by checking the box in the form.
  • Where it's stored: both the form and the list run in Notion (Notion Labs, Inc.), which processes the data for me.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe.
  • Unsubscribing: just reply to any of my emails, or write to mjmates@email.cz. After that you won't get anything. I keep your email only with a note that you don't want to receive anything, so nothing reaches you again by mistake.

Sourcing map

The places on the sourcing map come from the public registers of the Státní veterinární správa, or SVS (Czech State Veterinary Administration): raw milk sellers and animal product processors registered for direct sale. I show businesses and farms with their name and address exactly as the SVS publishes them. Places in Slovakia come from the official lists of the Štátna veterinárna a potravinová správa SR, or ŠVPS (Slovak State Veterinary and Food Administration), and I show them the same way. Raw milk vending machines, dairy shops and farm shops that aren't in the register I add from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL license); this data is in a separate file, osm.json, under the same license. Private breeders are shown only by municipality, without name or street.

Are you on the map and don't want to be, or is something wrong? Email me at mjmates@email.cz and I'll fix or remove it within a few days.

What you can do about it

You can do any of the following, just write to me at mjmates@email.cz:

  • File a complaint with the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Czech Office for Personal Data Protection), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7.
