My story

If I could, why not you?

First I'd like to say that I was a really unconventional kid from a young age. I always knew that if I wanted something, I'd get it, because I'd simply do whatever it took to get there. As soon as I started eating normal food as a little kid and wasn't only on breast milk anymore, I started refusing meat on my own, without my parents pushing me into it. I even remember moments when I literally burst into tears when I saw dead animals on TV and stuff like that. I wasn't even on a typical vegetarian diet. I ate pretty much nothing but white bread and processed cocoa puddings. That diet lasted me all the way until I was fifteen. At fifteen I started really going all in on smoking weed, smoking cigarettes and drinking. I mean, I'd done it before too, but at fifteen it took off big time.

With this “rebel” lifestyle I was constantly, subconsciously looking for freedom.

Around fifteen, feminine traits started showing up in me big time: wide hips, fat mostly on my belly, no muscle, a soft, squeaky voice. I dressed and acted like a girl, too. Because my hormones were out of whack, I constantly had no energy, I was tired, and my moods went up and down like a seesaw. But what wrecked me the most was that even at fifteen I still looked like a little boy and I didn't even have sperm, not until I started eating meat. I was basically infertile.

When I was finishing middle school at fifteen, I was 167 cm and weighed barely 55 kilos. I was the third smallest guy in my class, and even some of the girls were bigger. My skin was unhealthily pale, I could hardly tan at all, my bones were weak, my joints hurt nonstop and I always had some injury. I felt weak, malnourished and completely aimless. Among the guys I was more of a submissive type, I never stood out at anything.

When I was 16, I started going to the gym, mostly because I was really skinny and people laughed at me. But I just couldn't get enough protein from the diet I was on back then, so I started eating meat and anything I could get my hands on: fast food, McDonald's, pizza, protein shakes. And not just a little. I was seriously overeating, easily 4,000 to 5,000 calories a day.

I started putting on weight like crazy. In one year I gained 40 kg and grew 12 cm.

This is me exactly one year later, at 182 cm and 95 kg, in my last month of being 16.

It was the first thing in my life where I really felt like I was moving forward. I could actually see results: I was growing faster than most guys my age. But honestly… almost all of it went into fat, and my health got pushed completely aside.

Sleep? Totally neglected. Alcohol on weekends, weed and cigarettes every day. I didn't realize it back then, but today I know that alcohol and marijuana are some of the most poisonous things there are. They wreck your body, they dehydrate you at the cellular level… and above all they keep you down: in low consciousness, in passivity, in illusions. Like it disconnects you from yourself.

Even though my diet back then was definitely better than not eating meat at all, honestly, I knew almost nothing about nutrition. The only thing I cared about was getting 200 grams of protein in a day. I didn't care where it came from, as long as the numbers added up. So I ate real junk: pizza, pasta, curd cheese, protein shakes, fast food full of seed oils… anything, as long as I kept growing.

By then I'd been training for about a year, but I'd developed brutal chronic inflammation in my joints. Constant pain in my elbows and wrists, and it wasn't even from the gym, it was from excess uric acid in my body. Every day my back hurt, and my knees hurt so much I couldn't even do a bodyweight squat.

Because of the pain I often had to take breaks from the gym. I went to a doctor with my knees. He told me everything was fine. I went to another one. He said the same thing. And meanwhile I was in pain every day, tired, with no energy, zero motivation for life and totally lost. It was dark in my head and I had no idea what I was doing wrong.

About half a year later, in the summer, when I was 17 and we were on vacation in Spain, I went for a casual run, 5 km. Right after I finished, my left ankle totally seized up. No fall, no bad step, nothing. It suddenly swelled up, turned completely purple, and I couldn't walk for the next 4 to 5 days. It took me a year to fully recover. All it ever took was walking, say, just 8 km during the day, and by evening the ankle hurt again, and I was out of commission for another 3 days. (Not that I could handle anything more demanding back then :D)

And as if that wasn't enough, a few months later my acne exploded. A total breakout. Mentally it was completely destroying me. I tried everything: expensive skincare, red light lamps, celery juice, estheticians, all kinds of detox cleanses... and nothing. It only got worse. Until it hit me that all those chemical products were actually just clogging up my body even more.

Totally at rock bottom, mentally and physically exhausted.

I went to a doctor for blood tests because I really had no idea anymore what was going on with me, and she was in total shock. She told me I had hyperuricemia, a raised uric acid level, as high as a 60-year-old man's. At the same time I had my testosterone checked too, and that was at rock bottom as well.

It all started to make sense. When there's too much uric acid in the body, it turns into sharp crystals that settle in the joints and cause brutal pain: gout. They can also build up in the kidneys and form stones. So it wasn't the gym. My body was simply crying for help.

Of course, conventional medicine immediately claims it's caused by high-purine foods, and what does that mean? Supposedly red meat. So at first I got scared, cut back on meat, quit drinking and smoking too. But somewhere deep down I felt this just didn't make sense. How could meat, something we humans have eaten for thousands of years, suddenly be toxic to the body?

But even after I quit those extremes, my metabolism was still totally out of whack. I ate a standard diet and focused only on protein, calories and the numbers on the bar. And that's how, at eighteen, I ended up weighing 106 kilos at 182 cm. I was seriously overweight, but I still thought I was actually healthy because I lifted more than most people at the gym.

Right after switching to the right diet, the inflammation stopped, and my uric acid and urea went back to normal. My testosterone shot up, my metabolism got back to normal, I dropped 15 kilos and looked 10 times better. Since then I've been happier than ever.

A 4-MONTH DIFFERENCE!

Hormones have an unreal effect on a person and play a key role in how their looks, organ health, metabolism and mind develop.

How I know this

I didn't study this in school. I went to the sources.

I didn't study microbiology, virology or medicine in college. Everything I teach, I studied on my own.

School teaches what a field claims today. I wanted to know where it came from and whether it holds up. That's why I read the original papers today's textbooks are built on, including the ones I disagree with. Along with them, peer-reviewed studies and authors who went against the current.

When it comes to what actually matters for your health, meaning why the body does what it does and what to give back to it, I know my way around better than most people who studied these fields. Not because I'm smarter. I just didn't stop at the textbook.
